🎯 Strategic Selection Criteria

What separates a winning "Software-Only" project from a generic CRUD app

🧠 Advanced Algorithmic Core

Project must center on a sophisticated automation engine — Computer Vision, NLP/RAG, Complex Optimization, or Predictive Modelling. Not a simple form-based website.

📱 Accessible Data Input

No hardware allowed. Inputs come from: smartphone cameras, voice recordings, satellite APIs, web scraping, or public datasets.

🇮🇳 High Societal Visibility

Problems impacting everyday citizens, farmers, government budgets, or public safety carry the highest weight in SIH evaluations.

🚀 Demo-ability in 5 Minutes

For Round 1 (PPT + Prototype), the idea must produce a visually impressive demo that judges remember. Pure backend logic won't win.

01
AgriTech Farmer Welfare
AI-Powered Mobile Grain Quality Grading & Fair Price Estimation
Target: Agriculture, Mandis, and Farmer Welfare  |  Feasibility: 8.5/10  |  Impact: 9.5/10
🔴 The Problem
At agricultural mandis, grain quality checking is done manually by traders who visually estimate impurities. Traders exaggerate defects (chaff, broken grains, discoloration) to buy crops at under-rates, systematically exploiting farmers who have no scientific verification tool.
🟢 The Solution
A mobile app where the farmer spreads grain on a dark sheet, snaps a photo. AI performs instance segmentation to count and classify healthy grains, broken grains, discolored grains, and dust/stones. Cross-references with government MSP and live market rates to output a fair price per quintal. Works fully offline.

02
Public Health Pharma
Deep-Learning Counterfeit Medicine Packaging Detection Engine
Target: Healthcare, Consumer Protection, Pharma  |  Feasibility: 8.0/10  |  Impact: 9.5/10
🔴 The Problem
Counterfeit medicines plague Indian pharmacies. Fake packaging looks identical to the naked eye, leading to treatment failures and preventable deaths. Normal consumers cannot distinguish genuine from counterfeit at the pharmacy counter.
🟢 The Solution
A verification app scans medicine strip packaging using a smartphone camera. AI analyzes micro-printing defects, font kerning shifts, logo misalignment, and packaging texture profiles that differ when counterfeiters duplicate original printing plates. Cross-references batch numbers against a secure registry.

03
Road Infrastructure Smart Cities
Crowdsourced Pothole Detection & Municipal Work-Order Automation
Target: Road Transport, Municipal Corporations  |  Feasibility: 8.5/10  |  Impact: 9.0/10
🔴 The Problem
Indian roads are notorious for potholes causing thousands of fatal accidents annually. Municipalities rely on slow manual inspections or reactive citizen complaints, leading to massive delays. There's no systematic, real-time way to detect and track road damage.
🟢 The Solution
A background SDK running in driver/delivery apps. When the phone is dashboard-mounted, it uses the camera + accelerometer to auto-detect potholes and geo-tag them. Thousands of reports from different vehicles are clustered into confirmed locations on a live GIS map, automatically generating municipal repair tickets.

04
Environment Agriculture
Satellite-AI Stubble Burning Logistics Marketplace & Smart Allocation Engine
Target: Environment, Agriculture, Energy  |  Feasibility: 7.5/10  |  Impact: 9.5/10
🔴 The Problem
Crop residue burning (parali) in North India causes deadly winter smog in Delhi-NCR. Farmers burn stubble because manual removal is expensive and they lack coordination to sell it to biomass/biogas companies. There's no logistics platform connecting supply (farmers) to demand (industry).
🟢 The Solution
An automated supply-chain marketplace. Satellite remote sensing predicts harvest dates and estimates stubble volume on fields. The platform coordinates local aggregators (baler operators) to collect, pack, and transport biomass to buying plants using optimized vehicle routing within the short 30-day harvest window.

Emergency Services Smart Cities
Autonomous Ambulance Traffic Optimization & Green Corridor API
Target: Urban Traffic, Emergency Medicine  |  Feasibility: 7.0/10  |  Impact: 9.0/10
🔴 The Problem
Traffic congestion in Indian cities causes severe ambulance delays, often leading to patient deaths during transit. Traffic police manually clearing roads is slow and cannot coordinate across multiple junctions simultaneously. Every minute counts.
🟢 The Solution
Software that integrates ambulance GPS with city Intelligent Traffic Management Systems. It calculates the ambulance's path, dynamically adjusts traffic light timings (signal preemption) along the route to create an automated "green corridor" — turning signals green before the ambulance arrives and restoring normal cycles after it passes.

Utilities Jal Shakti
Municipal Water Distribution Leakage & Theft Analytics Dashboard
Target: Water Resources, Municipal Utilities  |  Feasibility: 8.0/10  |  Impact: 8.5/10
🔴 The Problem
Up to 40% of piped water in Indian municipalities is lost to pipe leakage and illegal tapping before reaching households. This causes severe drinking water shortages. Municipalities cannot locate leaks without expensive, slow physical excavation.
🟢 The Solution
A diagnostic software platform ingesting flow rate and pressure telemetry from existing bulk meters at key distribution nodes. AI detects abnormal pressure drop signatures and pinpoints leak/theft coordinates by modeling hydraulic water head loss using physics-informed machine learning, without digging up streets.

Procurement Anti-Corruption
GeM Bid Cartel & Price Manipulation Detection Engine
Target: Public Procurement, Government e-Marketplace  |  Feasibility: 8.0/10  |  Impact: 9.0/10
🔴 The Problem
On government bidding portals (GeM), vendors form cartels. They coordinate bid prices to inflate costs, taking turns winning at artificially high rates. This drains crores of public taxpayer money. Manual auditing of thousands of tenders is impractical.
🟢 The Solution
An automated risk-scoring engine monitoring GeM tender/bidding records. It detects collusion by analyzing bidding timelines, price spreads, and hidden corporate connections (shared directors, same registered addresses). Graph Neural Networks classify suspicious vendor sub-graphs that bid together too frequently.

Public Safety Women's Safety
Crowdsourced Safety Analytics & CCTV Behavioral Anomaly Monitor
Target: Public Safety, Police, Women Welfare  |  Feasibility: 7.0/10  |  Impact: 9.5/10
🔴 The Problem
Women's safety in public spaces remains a major concern. CCTV cameras exist but nobody watches feeds 24/7. By the time footage is reviewed, the crime has already happened. An SOS button is useless if the victim can't reach their phone. Real-time automated threat detection is critical.
🟢 The Solution
An intelligent computer vision layer connecting to existing CCTV feeds. It automatically flags anomalous behavior — a person being stalked across multiple cameras, physical struggles, crowd clustering at odd hours. Sends instant alerts with camera location to nearest police patrols. Automatically blurs non-threat faces for privacy compliance.
